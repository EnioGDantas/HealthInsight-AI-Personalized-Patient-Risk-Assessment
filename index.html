HealthInsight AI

How It Works

HealthInsight AI collects basic patient information (demographics, history, lifestyle and measurements) and screens it for chronic disease risk: obesity, hypertension, diabetes and cholesterol. The current prototype is rule-based. It uses published clinical guidelines (CDC, ADA, ACC/AHA) and the validated ADA Type 2 Diabetes Risk Test. Machine-learning models are on the roadmap and will be added after they are validated on public health datasets.

⚠️ Prototype for demonstration only. It is not a medical device and does not provide a diagnosis. Please do not enter real patient data. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is stored or sent.
